Core Values

Three bearings I navigate by.

Bearing 01

Obsessive Growth

Over the years, I’ve learned that I’m at my worst when I’m idle. When I stop moving, I get comfortable, and comfort slowly turns into stagnation.

Growth doesn’t have to be dramatic. It can be a new skill, a hard lesson, or a shift in perspective.

CommitmentTo be a little wiser and a little better today than I was yesterday.

Bearing 02 · Newest

Agency

I move forward with the most clarity when I decide what success means to me, rather than borrowing someone else’s definition. That’s true in school, at work, and in my personal life.

CommitmentTo take ownership of my own direction. When I know what I’m working toward and why, the smaller decisions become much easier to make.

Bearing 03

Intentionality with Others

I believe in “everything in moderation, including moderation.” My ambitions are big, and I hold myself to a high standard. Still, I’ve learned that I do my best work when I’m not doing it alone.

CommitmentTo be thoughtful about the people I surround myself with: the ones I learn from, work alongside, and support. My ambitions matter, but I won’t pursue them at the expense of the relationships that help make them possible.
